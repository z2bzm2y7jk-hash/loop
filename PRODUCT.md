# Loop
## Platform
Mobile-first web prototype, with a future iPhone application.
## Stack
Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Lucide. Local state and local storage only.
## Product
Golfers track scores and side games on the course and calculate peer-to-peer settlements. No funds are held, processed, or transferred. The complete provided brief is authoritative. The user explicitly delegates product decisions and requests no clarification pauses.
## Users and workflows
Four friends, Chad, Mike, John and Steve, set up a round, enter scores, review game balances, simulate golf and settle after play. Large outdoor-friendly controls and realistic seeded histories are required.
## Assumptions
Team Nassau uses best-ball gross score with equal teams; individual mode plays all pairs. Skins stakes are per opponent. Unwon final skins expire. Prototype variants must explain their rules.
