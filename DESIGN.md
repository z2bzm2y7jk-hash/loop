# Loop design
Mode: Operate. The user delegated visual decisions in the detailed brief.
Direction: clubhouse scorebook. Warm off-white paper, forest green actions, quiet olive secondary elements, editorial serif headings and compact sans-serif labels. Persistent desktop rail becomes a five-action mobile bottom bar with an emphasized Play action. Home has an asymmetric invitation and season summary; live scoring prioritizes four large score controls over decoration.
Candidates considered: printed scorecard, course wayfinding, golf editorial, clubhouse scorebook, tournament pairing sheet, yardage book, pro-shop receipt. The assigned fourth direction informed the implementation. The concept service was unavailable; the direction used the supplied product brief without external challengers.
Tokens: paper #f7f8f2, green #203f33, ink #263a31, accent #d8e8aa. Border-separated lists; mostly 6–12px control and panel radii, with 16px dialogs and 17px mobile sheet corners. No remote imagery or font services. Georgia and Arial use local system fonts; the app still requires a running Next.js server and does not provide an offline service worker.
Responsive behavior: the rail narrows at 1100px and becomes bottom navigation at 760px. Scoring uses a horizontal player strip; additional players scroll into view. Rules and simulation dialogs become bottom sheets on mobile. Scorecards scroll horizontally with sticky player names.
Interaction styling: green focus outlines, lime text selection, green input carets, tabular score numerals, subdued disabled controls, and offset soft shadows on dialogs and toast messages. A desktop hero entrance uses a short upward motion and fade; reduced-motion preferences disable animation and transitions.

Feature extension: selected game cards now lead to explicit dollar-and-cent settings, using bordered disclosure rows. This keeps multiple game options scannable. In-play decisions live below the score controls, with a small jump target near the hole heading. Wolf choices are pill-shaped large tap targets; Hammer offers show the current team stake and response options; Games status records the outcome by hole. Existing score entry and color hierarchy remain the visual priority.
